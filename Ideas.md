# Ideas

[[Home]] · [[Tasks]] · [Capture today](obsidian://daily?vault=Obsidian)

Add `#idea` to a thought worth revisiting, wherever you wrote it. This list finds tagged notes and shows matching context; open a result to read or develop the idea. Plain, untagged thoughts remain in your daily notes.

```query
tag:#idea -path:"4 Archive/" -path:"6 Templates/" -path:"3 Resources/Notetaking/" -path:"Ideas.md"
```

Once a week, choose a promising idea: link it to an active project, turn it into a concrete task, or leave it to incubate. An idea is only a commitment when you make it a checkbox.
